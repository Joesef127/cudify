# **PRIVACY POLICY**

## **Introduction**

Cudi Technologies Limited (“**Cudify**”, “**we**”, “**us**”, “**our**”) is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal data when you use our website, mobile applications, APIs, and related services (collectively, the “**Services**”).

Where required, we act as the **Data Controller** for personal data processed through our Services.

This Policy is drafted in compliance with:

* Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)  
* EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)  
* UK GDPR

**Personal Data We Collect**

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

### **a. Identity Data**

* Full name  
* Date of birth  
* Government-issued identification (e.g., NIN for Nigerian users)  
* Bank Verification Number (BVN)  
* Selfies or biometric verification data (where required)

### **b. Contact Data**

* Email address  
* Phone number  
* Residential address

### **c. Financial & Transaction Data**

* Bank account or wallet details  
* Transaction history  
* Payment references and amounts

### **d. Technical & Usage Data**

* Device information  
* IP address  
* Log data  
* Cookies and analytics data

### **e. Compliance Data**

* KYC/KYB records  
* AML/CFT screening results  
* Sanctions and watchlist checks

## **How We Use Your Data**

We process personal data for the following purposes:

* To provide and operate our Services  
* To verify identity and meet regulatory obligations  
* To process transactions and payments  
* To prevent fraud and financial crime  
* To communicate with users  
* To improve and optimise our products  
* To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

## **Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR & NDPR)**

We process personal data based on one or more of the following lawful grounds:

* **Contractual necessity** \- to provide Services  
* **Legal obligation** \- AML, CFT, tax, and regulatory compliance  
* **User consent** \- where explicitly obtained  
* **Legitimate interest** \- service improvement, fraud prevention

## **Data Sharing & Disclosure**

We may share personal data with:

* Regulated financial institutions and banking partners

* KYC, AML, and fraud prevention providers

Your personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the UK, EU, Nigeria, Canada and other jurisdictions.

Where required, we ensure appropriate safeguards such as:

* Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

## **Data Retention**

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to:

## **Your Data Protection Rights**

### **Under NDPR (Nigeria)**

You have the right to:

* Access your personal data  
* Correct inaccurate data  
* Withdraw consent where applicable  
* Request deletion, subject to legal obligations  
* Lodge complaints with the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**

### **Under GDPR / UK GDPR**

You have the right to:

* Access your personal data  
* Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data  
* Withdraw consent where applicable  
* Request erasure of personal data  
* Restrict or object to processing  
* Data portability  
* Lodge complaints with the **UK ICO** or relevant EU Data Protection Authority
